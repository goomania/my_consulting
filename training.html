Workshop formats

Programs sized for executives, managers, and teams.

Executive AI Briefing

Fast, decision-oriented sessions for leaders who need a grounded view of AI opportunities, limits, and risk.

  • What AI is changing in operations and knowledge work
  • Where value is realistic in the next 90 days
  • Risk posture and governance starter questions
  • Leadership decisions needed for momentum

Use Case Discovery Workshop

Working sessions that help teams map repetitive tasks, decision bottlenecks, and candidates for AI-enabled redesign.

  • Workflow mapping and pain-point analysis
  • Use case generation and prioritization
  • Value and feasibility discussion
  • Initial implementation roadmap

Role-Based Team Enablement

Practical training for the people who need to use AI consistently in daily work.

  • Prompting for real tasks and recurring outputs
  • Workflow redesign and review habits
  • Guardrails, quality control, and escalation
  • Manager expectations and adoption metrics

Curriculum themes

Training built around how organizations actually adopt new capabilities.

The goal is not just AI literacy. It is organizational readiness: shared language, useful habits, management expectations, and a practical sense of where AI should and should not be used.

01

AI fundamentals for business teams

Explain the landscape, likely use cases, common mistakes, and realistic expectations.

02

Prompting and workflow design

Move beyond one-off prompting into repeatable workflows, templates, and review patterns.

03

Risk, trust, and responsible use

Clarify boundaries around sensitive information, quality control, and policy alignment.

04

Adoption and management practices

Equip leaders and managers to reinforce new habits instead of hoping usage happens organically.
